call transcript, result/history and necessary delivery status; determine the

implementation.

previous proposed 12-month requests retention. The proposed 30/90-day and other
periods are not approved policy either.

cleanup. Calendar events and delivered CRM copies remain controlled by the owner/
recipient. Deleting TalkHint history must not automatically cancel events or
delete CRM copies. Calendar cancellation is a separate explicitly authorized
operation. Unknown legal identity, contacts, liability terms and no-training
claims stay decision blockers, not invented facts.
