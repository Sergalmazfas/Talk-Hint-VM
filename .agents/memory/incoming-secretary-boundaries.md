---
name: Incoming business Secretary boundaries
description: Owner-approved first-stage request intake and disclosure limits for future business incoming calls.
---

Incoming Work Secretary is a separate opt-in capability, not permission to
change manual/personal reception, outgoing voices, forwarding or SMS.
Creating business cards or connecting a Work number does not activate AI reception.

**Why:** The owner authorized preparation and isolated testing, explicitly
excluding publication, activation on a real number and real calls at this stage.

**How to apply:** Require a separately approved release and explicit activation.
Keep client disclosure permission distinct from Shared visibility: only explicitly
authorized, owner-owned Work facts may enter the incoming agent.

The first business is future mobile massage at the client's address, not an
already launched operation. Without a real calendar, collect requests only;
the owner confirms the final time. An approved request is never a calendar booking.

**Why:** The owner has not determined prices, session lengths, service area or
cancellation rules and requires an explicitly chosen business timezone.

**How to apply:** Never infer commercial facts or availability. Preserve partial
requests, corrections, original-language text and timezone meaning, independently
of whether owner push delivery succeeds.

Direct-audio model tool execution and auxiliary ASR completion need not occur
in transcript order. Do not make completed client review depend on late ASR.

**Why:** A late transcript after a successfully reviewed intake must not be
mistaken for a new caller correction; a real new utterance must still invalidate
unprocessed review before further tool execution.

**How to apply:** Treat speech-start and final transcription as different events;
test reordered events explicitly when changing intake review semantics.
