name: Client product modes
description: Owner-approved three-mode TalkHint client scope and separate onboarding/channel consent requirements.
---

One TalkHint product, three client modes:
- Speak yourself: transcript/history, hints and text translation with independent toggles.
- Speak through a voice translator: selected RU/ES ↔ EN.
- Secretary: incoming calls under preconfigured rules and separately confirmed outgoing assignments.

Copilot is excluded from the client-version plan; do not remove its existing
interface, code or data without a separate instruction. Shared permitted cards,
call goal and history remain common. Price information can be in permitted
materials; a complex industry catalog is outside the launch scope.

The first validation audience is independent professionals and small businesses
with a language barrier, without restricting by immigration status.

Registration must plan explicit versioned Terms acceptance and accessible
Privacy before number assignment; authority to represent the business; separate
Calendar/CRM/microphone permissions; separate SMS-recipient and conversation-
participant consents. Owner Terms acceptance is not consent on their behalf.

**Why:** The owner explicitly set this client-product direction on 2026-10-22.
The earlier Secretary-only landing brief describes that first page, not the
complete future client product.

**How to apply:** Keep these as product/onboarding requirements until their
roadmap stages are authorized. The provider audit is not authorization to change
models/routing, implement onboarding or remove existing UI. Never equate planned
language combinations or controls with completed live validation.
