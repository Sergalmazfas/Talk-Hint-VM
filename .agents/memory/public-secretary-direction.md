The owner chose the teal visual direction and explicitly rejected the blue one.

marketing Copilot, Translator and manually assisted calling on this first page.

**Why:** The user approved this as a continuation of the existing product, not
a parallel customer portal or a resurrection of the cancelled SMS-development task.

**How to apply:** Use one account and the existing permitted business card as the
source of business facts. No duplicate business questionnaire or directory.
Demo audio must be an explicitly approved public recording; decorative waves,
illustrative transcripts and planned microphone testing must not imply real
playback or a completed personal test. Do not promise pricing, free-ChatGPT
availability, customer messages, booking or verified legal/support details
without evidence. Real-number activation is a separate explicit future action.
