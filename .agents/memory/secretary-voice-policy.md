After personally rating Retell + TalkHint Engine highly in the browser bench, the owner explicitly approved adding it to telephone Secretary and the existing Swift/Xcode admin app, making it the native default while retaining a GPT choice. This supersedes the former GPT-Live-only Secretary policy. Browser approval is not proof of PSTN/iPhone operation.

**Why:** The user said Retell had passed their bench, authorized telephone testing, and requested its existing settings and priority in the native Secretary.

**How to apply:** Retell + Engine is the new native Secretary default; explicit GPT remains selectable. Do not silently reroute old approved attempts, historical providers, omitted-provider integrations, or incoming-number routing. Hint, Translator and Copilot remain independent. Retell must use the real confirmed assignment, not browser TEST fixtures. No real call or publication is authorized merely by permission to implement the connection.

Older native clients may explicitly send cloned-voice choices. Require a fresh preparation under the current Secretary choice rather than silently rerouting a signed old request. Native Settings must clearly identify the Secretary model separately from Translator/Copilot.

**Why:** Overriding an explicit client value would silently change the caller's selected voice, including already approved requests.

**How to apply:** Keep Secretary selection independent of the Translator/Copilot clone preference; do not infer the Secretary preference from that older shared value. Preserve attempted draft choices on retries. Distinguish prepared native changes from installed builds: they require a separate iOS update. A Dev or handshake check is not authorization to publish or proof that a PSTN call succeeded.

The owner wants the standard introduction to say “secretary”, not “assistant” or “AI assistant”, across native calls, ChatGPT-originated calls and every voice option. Answer direct questions about AI truthfully and preserve applicable mandatory notices.

**Why:** The owner confirmed that a real call succeeded but objected to the spoken role being “assistant”.

**How to apply:** Use only an explicitly confirmed account-bound owner name; never infer a full identity from a short phone label, transfer another owner's name, or accept identity changes from stale task wording. Per-response introduction instructions must retain the full shared identity and safety policy, rather than replacing it with an older greeting.

## IVR pilot duration and owner assistance

The owner superseded the earlier five-minute choice on 2026-10-29: telephone Secretary calls now have a ten-minute limit. Waiting for an owner's menu choice does not authorize extending that limit.

**Why:** The owner explicitly approved “предел звонка Кати — 10 минут вместо 5” after Claude's IVR review.

**How to apply:** Do not promise indefinite IVR waiting. Explain the ten-minute boundary; a longer call limit needs a new owner decision. Browser-only benches keep their independent limits. Server-only menu assistance must not be described as a native iOS text-input action or as verified push delivery without testing those paths.
