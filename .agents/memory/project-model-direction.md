The owner wants an internal model of TalkHint, not a universal builder or an
end-user business questionnaire. Business thinking starts at the startup layer
and descends into capabilities, processes, actions, responsibilities, rules,
procedures and requirements. Gantt is not the foundation.

**Why:** The owner said “Я бы сейчас не заморачивался с конструктором” and
explicitly requested the model for detailed work on TalkHint with ChatGPT.

**How to apply:** Keep proposals separate from decisions, and content confidence
separate from implementation readiness. Crew represents obligations, not
automatically executing agents. Future ChatGPT model tools need separate approval.

TalkHint is B2B; its buyer is the business owner. Its own model is distinct from
models of businesses it serves. The owner subsequently broadened the agreed
vision to an AI manager of a small service business with specialist Crew:
secretary is one role, not the whole target product. The owner retains approval
and control; detailed authority belongs in later procedures, not implied autonomy.
This is agreed target positioning, not evidence that these functions exist.

The US is the target market, starting research in Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm
Beach. No winning segment has been selected. Massage is an example; cleaning is
the next research candidate alongside beauty and other small service trades.
The stationary salon remains a separate illustrative client model.

**Why:** The owner explicitly updated the startup layer and requested competitor
benchmarking. Earlier selection of a stationary salon must not be treated as the
current winning market segment.

**How to apply:** Do not propagate the illustrative salon's resources, prices,
capacity or assumptions into real customers' cards or the general phone agent.
The salon's $20 per session is revenue before expenses, not profit; conditional
price/capacity examples are not actual utilization or market research.
