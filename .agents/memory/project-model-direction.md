The owner wants an internal model of TalkHint, not a universal builder or an
end-user business questionnaire. Business thinking starts at the startup layer
and descends into capabilities, processes, actions, responsibilities, rules,
procedures and requirements. Gantt is not the foundation.

**Why:** The owner said “Я бы сейчас не заморачивался с конструктором” and
explicitly requested the model for detailed work on TalkHint with ChatGPT.

**How to apply:** Keep proposals separate from decisions, and content confidence
separate from implementation readiness. Crew represents obligations, not
automatically executing agents. Future ChatGPT model tools need separate approval.

Keep independent product hypotheses, not successive replacements of one model.
Neither has been chosen as the primary hypothesis. The original is TalkHint as
an AI manager of a small business with specialist roles; retain all its ten
blocks and research. The separate alternative is a multilingual AI assistant
for life and small business in a new country: one chat, overlapping personal/work
contacts, contact-linked calendar/history, one or two numbers and configurable
secretaries; WhatsApp later, no marketing-role team. Develop each block
independently; unresolved sections stay unknown, not inherited from the original.
Both describe target concepts, not proof that all functions work.

**Why:** the owner corrected the previous instruction: “две сохранённые
альтернативы вместо замены”. The original product must be recovered from actual
history, never reconstructed from memory or invented.

**How to apply:** viewing a hypothesis is not choosing a primary. Keep edits,
history and claims isolated; never automatically copy market/competitor claims
between alternatives or replace the massage/cleaning research.

The new-country hypothesis's agreed client framing includes both B2C personal
affairs and B2B self-employment/small service business. Initially the user and
payer are the individual or business owner, who may use both modes. Massage
therapists, cleaners and repair workers are examples to test, not audience limits.
Demand and willingness to pay remain unconfirmed. The problem framing is based
on the founder's personal experience, not evidence from market research.

**Why:** the owner approved the client/problem framing as hypotheses and explicitly
identified personal experience as the problem's source, not validated market
evidence or a replacement of the original hypothesis's pilot segment.

**How to apply:** preserve both purchase scenarios and the unvalidated status;
do not import the original pilot's geography or competitors into this alternative.

The new-country solution combines language support, an action-taking secretary
and personal/business organization through one AI assistant. Context comes from
connected mail and explicitly permitted calendars; the owner decides which
actions may be autonomous and which require confirmation. Intended value is
less organizational time, less dependence on others and more control over
agreements; these are unmeasured targets, not verified effects.

**Why:** the owner approved this solution framing while explicitly distinguishing
the target concept from a report of working capabilities.

**How to apply:** do not treat planning text as a runtime permission grant,
mail/calendar connection or evidence that the complete workflows work.

For the new-country market slide, distinguish the supplied ACS language-audience
estimates from immigrants, customers, revenue or proven demand. The cohort is
adults with home Spanish/Russian and English below “very well”, regardless of
birthplace. Add only non-overlapping state estimates; NYC is inside New York
state and must not be added again. City figures cannot be replaced by counties,
metros or entire states. Keep exact values/method/source in an explanation and
round display values independently; sampling error has not been calculated.

**Why:** the owner explicitly limited this slide to existing comparable estimates
and prohibited invented city figures, penetration rates and working-number shares.

**How to apply:** do not claim a new microdata calculation or validation when only
formatting supplied figures; future city research must match cohort and geography.

For the original AI-manager hypothesis, the US is the target market, starting
in Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach.
The owner's confirmed decision dated 2026-10-23 selects independent licensed
massage therapists with their own small private practice and no administrator
for the first pilot. This is a segment to test, not proof of profitability,
low competition or willingness to pay. The stationary salon remains a separate
illustrative client model, not the parameters of this pilot.

MassageBook and ClinicSense are the pilot's primary competitors. All earlier
competitors, research, sources and history must remain in the general benchmark;
changing segment priority is not permission to delete or replace that work.
The owner explicitly corrected the goal: “Мы не делаем меньше участия владельца,
мы делаем удобный инструмент. Это разные вещи”. Reducing owner participation is
not the target. The two agreed evaluation axes are convenience of managing the
practice and impact on income after expenses. Both remain unmeasured, not proven
advantages; the owner stays involved and approves actions.
MBLEx is a potential audience channel, not a confirmed database of buyers.

**Why:** The owner explicitly narrowed the pilot and repeated “те конкурентов не
убирай ничего, потому что мы по ним делаем бенчмаркетинг”. Earlier broader
segment exploration and the fictional salon must not override this decision.

**How to apply:** Do not propagate the illustrative salon's resources, prices,
capacity or assumptions into real customers' cards or the general phone agent.
The salon's $20 per session is revenue before expenses, not profit; conditional
price/capacity examples are not actual utilization or market research.

The original hypothesis's proposed differentiation combines conversational practice management and
advertising/content Crew. The owner supplies original video; the hypothesis is
editing that video, not generating new scenes. Publication and advertising/budget
need separate approvals. End-to-end Meta advertising, attribution and increased
income are a target hypothesis, not working capabilities or guaranteed sales.
Competitors already have marketing, rebooking, documents and payments. Not
finding their full conversational editing/advertising chain in reviewed materials
does not prove that chain or AI is absent.

**Why:** These are explicit owner corrections and boundaries for the assembled
project package.

**How to apply:** Preserve earlier formulations in history, not as current goals.
Keep unmeasured coordinates/ratings/income unknown, and compare documented
capabilities with documented capabilities, not the TalkHint vision as finished.
