A few practical details before you get started.

Do I need an app?

No app download is needed for this experience. TalkHint is managed through ChatGPT, with the secretary and its history available there when the supported connection is set up.

Why do I need both an account and ChatGPT?

Your existing TalkHint account links to ChatGPT using its supported custom MCP connection and account-authorization flow. Availability depends on your ChatGPT plan and feature access; not every free account supports custom connections.

Can it answer incoming calls and make outgoing calls?

Both directions are part of the secretary concept. This website demo is illustrative only; it does not place or receive a real call, activate a phone number, or book appointments.

What if my business card doesn’t have the answer?

It should say it doesn’t have that information, rather than invent a policy, price, opening time, or promise. Keeping the verified card current is how you shape what it knows.

Is the browser test a real call?

No. A personal browser test is a test experience, not a telephone call. Safe microphone access and business-card editing tools are not yet connected to this public setup flow.

Which languages and voices are supported?

English, Russian and Spanish are intended for built-in language support. Language switching requires controlled live tests and is not a guarantee that every voice or call will work in each language. Voice choices depend on verified implementation capabilities.

Will it email me, text customers, or book my calendar?

Do not rely on those workflows. Owner notifications depend on availability; email, SMS and calendar booking are not promised as ready features here.
